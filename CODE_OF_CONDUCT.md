# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors must make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

Examples of expected behavior include using welcoming language, respecting differing
viewpoints, accepting constructive criticism, focusing on what is best for the
project, and showing empathy toward other community members.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and Enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual is representing the
project in public spaces. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or
temporarily or permanently exclude participants for behavior they determine to be
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
published contact method. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and in good faith.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
